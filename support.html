How to play

Deal
Tap DEAL. A 52-card deck is shuffled and split into two stacks of 26.
Battle
Tap PLAY CARD. Both sides turn their top card. The higher rank takes both cards to the bottom of its stack. Aces are high. Suits do not matter.
War
Equal ranks start a War. Each side plays one face-down card, then one face-up card. Repeat while ranks stay equal. The higher face-up takes the whole pot.
Win or lose
Hold all 52 cards to win. Hold zero cards and you lose. If a side cannot supply a required War card, that side loses.
Play again
When the match ends, tap PLAY AGAIN for a fresh deal.

Common questions

Does the game use data or show ads?
No. There are no ads, no in-app purchases, no account, and no data collection. See the privacy policy.
Can I play against another person on this phone?
No. The other stack is a local computer opponent that uses the same War rules.
