Privacy Policy

High Card War · Effective August 13, 2026

High Card War collects no personal data. There is no account, no sign-up, no analytics, no advertising identifier, and no third-party tracking code in the app.

What the app stores

High Card War keeps the current deal and battle state in memory for the session. None of it is transmitted to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app removes everything.

What we collect

Nothing. We operate no server that receives data from the app, and we have no way to identify you or your device. There are no ads, no in-app purchases, and no analytics SDKs.

Children

High Card War is rated for all ages. Because the app collects no data at all, it collects no data from children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the revised version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Any change that involves collecting data would ship with an app update and updated App Store privacy disclosures.
